The console terminal, before and after

What an agent's terminal can do today and what it should do: bigger, expandable, scrollable, easy to copy from, and usable on a phone. Static mocks; the terminal text is only a stand-in.

  • No size control, no expand, no full screen.
  • Copy: select with the mouse and press the shortcut; a long reply is cut by the box; no “copy the last answer”.
  • Scrollback: 2000 lines, by the wheel only; no key says “you are not at the bottom”.
  • Its width is shared with the page: ~50 columns.

  • Expand: the console takes the whole main area; Operations folds into a one-line rail of agent chips.
  • Full screen: the terminal alone (the browser's full screen), Esc to leave; keeps keyboard focus.
  • Copy has four modes and shows “Copied 38 lines”; selection also works with the mouse and Shift+drag when the program takes the mouse.
  • Latest appears when you scroll up; following says new output will scroll.
  • Scrollback 10 000 lines (a Flight Rules setting); Find searches it.
