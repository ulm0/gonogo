# Glossary

The words you meet in the first ten minutes.

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Room** | One gonogo with its own agents, mail, task board and settings. `main` is the first; the page at `/` lists them ([Rooms](../features/rooms.md)). |
| **Console** | One agent: its terminal, its tile in the room, its state. "Staff" a console means hire an agent. |
| **FLIGHT** | The boss agent, made at the first start. It takes your brief and hands out work. You are the **Director**. |
| **Brief** | A message you type in the box under the room; it goes to FLIGHT. |
| **Downlink** | Your inbox: what agents send you and what needs a go or no-go ([Downlink](../features/downlink.md)). **COMMS** (INBOX, TRAFFIC, THREADS) shows it and all other mail ([Comms](../features/comms.md)). |
| **Flight Rules** | The settings screen (round key with three sliders; [Flight Rules](../features/flight-rules.md)). **Patch Panel** (Cmd+K or Ctrl+K) jumps to any view or action. |
| **Propellant** | Token budgets that stop an agent or the room when used up ([Propellant](../features/propellant.md)). **Redline** stops loops and error storms ([Redline](../features/redline.md)). |
| **Livery** | The look of the page: colours, and an agent can design one ([Livery](../features/livery.md)). |
