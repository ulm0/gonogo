- **The full anchor grammar and a safe engine for it.** Claims can use multivac's anchor grammar (modes present, absent, unique, count=N, each, each!; globs; POSIX regex or a fixed `"literal"`), ported with its MIT notice, with extra refusals (a repeated group that holds a quantifier, a pattern over 200 characters, a glob that leaves the repository). Anchors run in a worker thread that is ended at 5 seconds, at most 2 workers per room and one run per checkout every 30 seconds, reading only tracked files through their real path. Nothing is blocked by a result; it is a hint. New dependency: picomatch 4.0.7.
