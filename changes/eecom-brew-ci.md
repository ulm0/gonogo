- **CI: the Homebrew formula is tried on macOS and Linux.** A workflow (`brew.yml`, for a pushed `v*` tag, by hand, and for a change of the formula) installs the formula from a throwaway tap on a macOS and a Linux runner, from the tarball of the checkout (`brew install --build-from-source`), runs `brew test` and `brew audit --strict --formula`, and checks that the prebuilt node-pty has its `spawn-helper` executable. Until now the formula had never been run with a real `brew`. No change for users.
