- **Knowledge store, proposals and tools (off until you turn it on).** gonogo keeps the claims you accept about a repository outside the repository, shared by the rooms of an install: identified by its remote and root commit, bound to the checkouts you approve. Agents can read the accepted claims (`knowledge_list`) and propose new ones (`knowledge_propose`); a proposal does nothing until you accept it, and the agent only hears "accepted" or "rejected". Routes for the Knowledge block, and a "move claims" action that asks for your password.
