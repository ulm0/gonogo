- **COMMS shows what waits on you, and you answer it there.** A "Needs you" strip sits above the COMMS tabs with a count: hire requests with GO and NO-GO, handoffs FLIGHT asks to send, and messages that ask you something (GO and NO-GO keys for a go/no-go, a reply box for a question). An answered one leaves the strip. A message counts as asking when it is unread and says go/no-go or has a question mark; "Not a question" clears one that only looks like it.
