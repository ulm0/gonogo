The room view is new: a status strip (the clock, activity, poll, Max Q and fuel as keys that open their panel), the staffed consoles first with the open seats in one row, and the selected console's terminal. The old wall of five boxes comes back as the "Full wall" density in the Layout popover (a later change); until then a room with a saved layout keeps its grid.
A stop that is refused because the room did not answer whether it is busy now says so in a field (`needsForce: true` in the 409), and the rooms page offers "Stop it anyway?" from that field, not from the words. A chip of the status strip is named by what it shows, and the T-minus chip is shown whenever a poll or static fire is open, whatever the layout.
The terminal no longer takes the keyboard when a room loads (xterm keeps Ctrl+K for itself, so Ctrl+K opened nothing until a click elsewhere): it takes the focus on a click, or on Enter on its console card. Flight Rules no longer rebuilds every block when a value arrives (the group was a new component at every render, so blocks lost what was typed in them and fetched again).
