- **CI: a release is checked after it is published.** A `verify` job in `release.yml` checks that npm's sha1 is the sha1 of the release's own tarball and that npm holds a provenance attestation, and a `clean-install` job installs the version in a clean `node:24` container (pinned by digest; waiting up to 20 minutes for npm) and checks that `gonogo --version` is the tag. No change for users.
