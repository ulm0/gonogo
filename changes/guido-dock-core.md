- **Docking 2, the core: a link.** A task handed to another station is now bound to a link both stations hold (`dl_…`); each side owns its own fields and the other only gets events (offer, accept, status, note), numbered per author, applied once, refused when rewritten, and kept in an outbox until the other station acks them (`POST /dock/link`, `dock-outbox.json`). A task handed off stays open here; the receiver's status changes and notes travel back by themselves, without the text for a task marked `localNotes`. Offers carry `due`, `priority`, `onward` and `budget`. A station takes at most 30 tasks an hour and 20 open ones from one dock and says why when it refuses. `GET /api/links` lists the links, `GET /api/links/<id>` the trail, `GET /api/docking/outbound?dock=` (the Director's own login) what left, exactly as it left.
