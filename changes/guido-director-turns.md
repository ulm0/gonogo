- **Your messages reach the agent as your turn.** When you write to an agent from a signed-in browser, or from your private Telegram chat, gonogo now also types your words into its console after the pointer (`gonogo: 1 new message from user. Call read_messages. [Director via web] go ahead`). Claude Code's auto mode treats what an agent reads from a tool as data, so a "go" in mail never authorized anything; typed as a turn it does. Never done for agents, stations, Uplinks, schedules, triggers or API keys; escape sequences, controls and line breaks are removed and the text is cut at 1,000 characters. On by default, off with *Director messages as user turns* in FEATURES. **Whoever holds your signed-in session or your Telegram chat speaks with your authority.** [Details](https://gonogo.ulm0.com/features/relay.html#your-words-as-a-user-turn).
