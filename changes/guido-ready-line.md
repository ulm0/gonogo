- **The `gonogo ready:` line gives the address people are meant to use.** With `GONOGO_PUBLIC_URL` (or `--public-url`) set, the ready and setup lines carry that address instead of the loopback one. In a container, one more line on stderr says to open the computer's own address and port from another machine and to allow that name with `GONOGO_ALLOWED_HOSTS`. The `gonogo ready:` line on stdout keeps its form for scripts.
