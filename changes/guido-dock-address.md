Docking: a dock's address can be changed without undocking (CHANGE ADDRESS on the dock row, `PATCH /api/docking/<id>`): the dock keeps its name and secret and the next call goes to the new address. An address may now end with `/r/<room>`, so a dock in a room other than main can be reached from another station. A dock that the other station does not know at its address now says so (the dock may be in another room there, or was removed) instead of "refused".
