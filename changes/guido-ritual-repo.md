- **A mission can take closing items from its repo.** A mission has an optional `repo` (the git top level of a folder an agent of the room works in; it defaults to its lead's, or to the boss's own folder when the boss makes it) and, when the repo has `.gonogo/ritual.md`, its lines are **added after** the room's template (the repo never replaces your lines; together at most 20) and labelled "from the repo". Lines from a repo are written by agents: they are cleaned and read only through the real path inside the repo. Only you set the repo of another agent, and only you tick items. **Going back to an older build** drops `repo` at the first save of `missions.json`, and items marked "from the repo" then read as the room's.
