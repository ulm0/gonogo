- **A closing ritual on Missions.** A mission can carry a short checklist for its ending, copied from `<home>/ritual.md` (or from `.gonogo/ritual.md` of the mission's repo) when the mission is made, up to 20 items. Only you tick the items; closing with items open works and the Downlink says how many were left. The repo of a mission must be the git top level of a folder an agent of the room works in, and lines from a repo file are cleaned and labelled "from the repo". It is printed, never a gate.
