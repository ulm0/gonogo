- **A closing ritual on Missions.** A mission can carry a short checklist for its ending, copied from the room's template (`ritual.md`, edited in Flight Rules) when the mission is made, up to 20 items. You add, edit, remove and tick items; the boss may pass lines when it makes a mission (shown as "set by" its name). Only you tick. Closing with items open works and the Downlink lists what was left. It is printed, never a gate.
