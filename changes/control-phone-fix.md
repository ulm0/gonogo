- **The top bar of a room fits a phone, and a room on the list opens with a tap on its card.** On a 360 to 390 px phone the bar of a room had grown past the screen when the version and the Rooms key joined it (the logo alone is 128 px at its old size), so keys could sit over each other. The logo is smaller on a phone, the Rooms key is a round 44 px key (four squares, named "Rooms" for a screen reader), the link lamp keeps only its light while the link is up (its word returns for "connecting" and "link down"), and the bar wraps to a second line instead of overflowing when a row cannot fit. On the list of rooms, a tap anywhere on a running room's card opens it, not only on its OPEN key. **The list of rooms was broken on a phone too** (seen in the 390 px screenshots): a phone rule meant for the dense tiles of the Room view also cut every card of the list to 64 px, so its OPEN key was drawn over the next block, and the empty-state text ran past its box and made the page 513 px wide. The dense rules now apply only inside the Room view's matrix, and a card's text wraps. A phone check in a real browser (`browser/phone.spec.ts`: 390 and 360 px, no key over another, no sideways scroll, a tap opens a room) runs with the smoke test in CI.
