Docking 2, S4 (missions and money): a mission can be offered to a docked station like a task (the receiver makes its own planned mission, labelled with where it came from, and reports counts of its tasks, never titles; tasks handed over from the mission land inside it), a station can tell the other what a task it holds cost (`reportCost`, off by default: dollars to the cent, an estimate, one an hour and one at delivery), and the cap a peer offers for a linked task is clamped ($0.50 to the room's own budget) and enforced by the receiver's own Propellant as a `links` budget that holds only that one task.
